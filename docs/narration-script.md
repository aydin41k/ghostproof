## Narration — approximately 120 words

Welcome to GhostProof. Three rooms. Three suspicious stories. One fictional ghost hiding a lie in each.

These claims began with real local Gemma runs and a small, separately reviewed set of NASA and National Park Service sources. Now they're cached: no model call waits behind your next click.

Choose the fabrication. Catch it, and the ghost weakens. Miss it, and the haunting grows.

The reveal shows more than a verdict: the false claim, its correction, an exact quote, and the containing passage. Ghost lore stays lore. Unexplained does not mean alien.

This browser-local game bundles inspectable answers, so it's for learning, not anti-cheat competition.

Clear all three rooms, banish the ghost, then replay. The useful habit survives the haunting: check what the source actually says.
